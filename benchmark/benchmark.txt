Bechmarking notes

Tests were performed on version 3.0.0 while in development.

The benchmark data were collected using the following procedure:
(1) A data set was generated for N random temperature, density pairs.
(2) The state() method was evaluated at the entire data set.
(3) The _argparse() method was called with (T,d), (T,p), and (s,p) from the 
    resulting data set.
(4) The state() method was called with (T,d), (T,p), and (s,p) from the 
    resulting data set.

In the plots, the colors are
    'k': (T,d)
    'b': (T,p)
    'r': (s,p)
the linestyles are
    '-': state()
    '--': _argparse()
In benchmark_mp, the substances are:
    '^': mp.N2 as mp2 
    's': mp.H2O as mp1
In benchmark_ig, the substances are:
    's': ig.air as igmix
    '^': ig.N2 as ig2
    'o': ig.CHF as ig1
