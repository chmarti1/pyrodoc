Donate to PYroMat

Why?

Support for these kinds of projects is harder to find than it used to be. Among other things, your support will be used to:

  • Compensate students and developers for the time they commit to the project;
  • Offset the server costs I usually pay out-of-pocket;
  • Help me buy back some of my time from my organization to work on this project.

You'll be helping to:

  • Dramatically expand the multi-phase substances included;
  • Complete development on a slim graphical interface with plotting support;
  • Give more support for developers who want hooks into the fast back-end methods.

Thank you, just for considering donating.

How Much?

Do you think you've gotten a latte's worth of enjoyment or a gallon of gas (or a liter of petrol) of mileage out of the software? It's up to you -- it all helps more than you can know. If I received $0.01 for every download from the Python Package Index, all of the project's financial needs would be met.

Whatever PYroMat is worth to you, thank you for donating.

Who?

PYroMat is a project that a handful of like-minded engineers and scientists have contributed to over the years. It is not an independent legal entity like a nonprofit or LLC. Donations are collected by Chris Martin, the project's founder and primary developer.

Thank you so much for donating.
